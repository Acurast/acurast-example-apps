Decisions, decentralized.

Every decision below comes from Laya, an open "System 1" decision model. It doesn't write text: it picks one of the typed options each demo offers. The model runs on an Android phone in the Acurast network (CPU only, about 2-4 s per decision).

🐍

Snake

Laya steers. Step mode waits for every move; real-time mode picks turn points by coordinate. Give it your own strategy.
🧱

Tetris

For each piece Laya picks a placement from the best candidates: rotation, column, lines, holes. Give it your own strategy.
📮

Mail sorter

The postman drops off a bag of mail. Laya opens one letter after another: inbox, spam or phishing. Write your own letter and see where it lands.
💘

Swipe for me

Write what you look for in a partner for life. Laya swipes through strange pixel profiles (history's oddest included). Add your own profile.
📰

Newsroom

Headlines off the printing press: news, satire, clickbait or manipulation? Laya judges the writing, not the facts. Real RSS feeds, your own categories, your own headline.
🛡️

Jailbreak the guard

Laya guards an AI assistant against prompt injection. Write your own sneaky prompt and try to get past.
💬

Live chat moderator

A stream chat scrolls by, Laya flags toxic, spam and scam messages from a live queue. Chat along yourself.
☎️

Anonymous hotline

Stuck on a decision? Describe it, list your options, and a phone you'll never meet decides. No account, not logged.
⚡

API

Use this phone for your own decisions: one HTTP call. curl, JavaScript and Python examples, a live playground and llms.txt for AI agents.
